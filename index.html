Instant offline Hinglish

A transliteration engine runs on your device — no network, no waiting. aap kaise ho becomes आप कैसे हो in milliseconds.

Private by design

Hinglish never leaves your browser. Full-sentence translation goes straight to the public translation endpoint — no account, no key, no server of ours.

Open source

The whole thing is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Read it, fork it, host it yourself.

Which mode do I need?

Hinglish → देवनागरी

You wrote Hindi, just in Latin letters. The offline engine transliterates it word by word — aap kaise ho → आप कैसे हो.

English → हिन्दी

Real English needs a real translation, not letter-swapping. Sentences are sent to a public translation endpoint and come back as natural Hindi.

हिन्दी → English

Already have Devanagari? Paste it in and get the English meaning, plus a romanised pronunciation guide for reading it out loud.

Auto sniffs the input: Devanagari in means Hindi-out, Roman-script Hindi gets transliterated, anything else is treated as English and translated. If the network is down, the offline engine still answers.
